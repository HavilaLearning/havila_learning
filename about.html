About Havila Learning

Learning should feel possible, practical and joyful.

Havila Learning creates practical educational resources that help families and educators turn everyday moments into opportunities to learn.

What we believe

Young children learn through relationships, conversation, play, movement and exploration. Useful learning resources should be clear enough to use immediately and flexible enough to adapt to different children.

Our focus

  • Practical learning activities
  • Screen-free ideas
  • Accessible materials
  • Resources for parents, caregivers and educators
  • Educational eBooks and free resources
