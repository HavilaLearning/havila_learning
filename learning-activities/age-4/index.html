🔤

Letter hunt around the house

Look for familiar letters together and turn everyday objects into a playful literacy game.

🔢

Count and match everyday objects

Count buttons, toys or kitchen items and match groups to simple numbers.

🧩

Build a repeating pattern

Use blocks, cups or small objects to create and continue simple patterns.

🔷

Shape detective

Find circles, squares, triangles and rectangles around the home and talk about their features.

🔬

Sink or float discovery

Test safe household objects together and talk about what you observe.

🎨

Draw and tell a story

Invite your child to draw a simple scene and explain the story behind the picture.

Learning works best when it feels natural

Talk, play, read, count, make and explore together. The goal is not to make every moment a lesson, but to notice opportunities for learning in everyday life.
